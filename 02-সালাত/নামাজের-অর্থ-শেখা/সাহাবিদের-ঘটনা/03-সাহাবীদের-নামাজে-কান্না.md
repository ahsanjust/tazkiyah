## 2. Background

The Companions (Sahaba) were known for their deep khushu in prayer. Their hearts were so connected to Allah that they would weep during recitation and dhikr.

---

## 3. Full Narration

### Hadith 1: Anas ibn Malik (RA)
Anas (RA) said:
> I never saw anyone whose cheeks were more stained with tears than the Messenger of Allah (SAW) -- until he met his Lord. And I never heard anyone whose voice was more beautiful in recitation than the Prophet (SAW).

### Hadith 2: Ibn Masud (RA)
Ibn Masud (RA) said:
> The Prophet (SAW) recited Surah At-Tawbah, and it was seventy ayat. I used to recite it during the time of Uthman (RA) -- and I would finish it in the time it takes to recite Al-Baqarah.

### Hadith 3: Abdullah ibn ash-Shikhkhir
Abdullah ibn ash-Shikhkhir (RA) said:
> I came to the Prophet (SAW) while he was praying -- and his chest was weeping, like the sound of a boiling pot.

---

## 4. Lessons

### **Weeping from Khushu**
The Companions did not weep from fear of punishment alone -- they weeped from love (hubb), hope (raja), and awe (khashyah) of Allah.

### **Connection to Allah**
Their hearts were so connected to the verses that tears came naturally -- this is the fruit of tadabbur (contemplation).

### **Beautiful Voice**
The Prophet's (SAW) voice was beautiful in recitation -- but it was his heart that made it beautiful, not just his vocal cords.
