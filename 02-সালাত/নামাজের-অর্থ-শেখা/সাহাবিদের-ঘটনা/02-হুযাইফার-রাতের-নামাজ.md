---

## 2. Background

Hudhayfah (RA) was one of the closest companions. He followed the Prophet (SAW) to the mosque at night and observed his prayer.

---

## 3. Full Narration

Hudhayfah (RA) said:

> I saw the Prophet (SAW) pray at night. He recited Surah Al-Baqarah -- one ayat after another. He paused briefly after each ayat.

> He would say Allahu Akbar and go into ruku. He paused briefly. Then he would say Sami Allahu liman hamidah and stand.

> In sujud he would say Subhana Rabbiyal A'la. He stayed in sujud longer.

> He completed Surah Al-Baqarah in one rakat.

---

## 4. Lessons

### **Brief Pause -- Itminan (Tranquility)**
The Prophet (SAW) paused after each ayat, after each movement. This is khushu -- thinking, feeling, absorbing.

### **Long Surah in One Rakat**
Completing Al-Baqarah in one rakat shows both length and quality of the Prophet's prayer.
